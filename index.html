💡 Tips ▾
  • Swipe an exercise card left to delete it. You get 5 seconds to undo.
  • Press and hold a card, then drag it to reorder.
  • Tap a set's number to mark it as a warm-up (W).
  • Tap the title row of a card, or the date bar, to fold it.
  • Pick an exercise you've done before and it fills in last time's sets.
  • Everything saves as you type. Complete workout just clears the form.
